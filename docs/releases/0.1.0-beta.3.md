# Kleari 0.1.0-beta.3

> **Installing for the first time, or on a new Mac?** Read
> [Read me before you install](../install.md#read-me-before-you-install) first. macOS warns you
> the first time you open Kleari: choose **Done** (never **Move to Trash**), then **Open
> Anyway** in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

The third beta of Kleari: your processor's temperature and fans, many more alerts with a
switch for each kind, a busier menu-bar panel, a lighter app, and a round of fixes. Everything
still happens on your Mac only: no account, no telemetry, nothing sent.

**Requires** a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later.

## Install or update

If you have an earlier beta, Kleari offers this update in Settings › About (or when it opens).
Otherwise download `Kleari_0.1.0-beta.3_aarch64.dmg` below, open it and drag Kleari into
Applications.

## What's new

- **Temperature and fans:** the System page shows your processor's temperature from the chip's
  own sensors (average and hottest), and your fans' speed with their range. Kleari only reads
  them; it never changes fan speeds.
- **More alerts:** nearly full (under 5%) and getting full (under 15%) startup disk, critical
  memory pressure, a processor busy for 15 minutes, battery low (20%) and critical (10%),
  fully charged, hot, near the end of its life or needing service, a new login item, many
  login items, and a reminder when you haven't run a cleanup for a week.
- **A switch for each kind of alert** in Settings › Notifications: Storage, Performance,
  Battery, Login items and Reminders. If you had alerts on, they all stay on.
- **Menu-bar panel:** the alerts that need your attention, network download and upload, the
  processor's temperature and fan speed, and when your Mac last started up and woke.
- **Delete permanently instead:** in Cleanup's preview, next to Quarantine, after its own
  confirmation. The items are checked and moved into quarantine as usual, then deleted from
  there; anything that can't be deleted stays in Quarantine.

## Fixed and changed

- Browser care showed "0 tabs in 0 windows" for every browser; tabs are listed again, and
  faster.
- CPU no longer shows "Measuring…" for long on the Dashboard, and System's readings no longer
  drop out while Kleari's window isn't in front.
- Battery health now shows the same maximum capacity as System Information.
- Sizes under 1 MB show in KB instead of "0 MB".
- A project at your home folder (a stray `npm install` there) can be cleaned; it no longer
  always counts as "Active".
- Lighter on your Mac: processes and live readings are read only while a page shows them,
  and the frosted scrolling effects are gone.
- After a cleanup, the pages your items came from scan again, so they don't list what's gone.
- Preview and approve, Maintenance and Files keep their headers and tabs in place and scroll
  only the list; nothing overlaps in a small window.

The full list is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Known limitations

- Kleari isn't notarised by Apple, so macOS asks you to allow it the first time.
- The processor's temperature and the fans are read through interfaces Apple doesn't
  document. A macOS update could stop them working; Kleari then says they aren't reported
  rather than showing a wrong value. Graphics temperature appears only on chips that name
  their graphics sensors (not the M4).
- macOS doesn't record when a Mac was shut down, and a restart looks the same as starting up,
  so the menu shows the last start-up.
- Alert thresholds are fixed; they can't be changed yet.
- Kleari can't tell when an app has stopped responding: macOS doesn't let apps find out.
